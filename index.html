Origami Wars — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Origami Wars is made by Breakthrough Games. This policy explains what the game does with your information. It applies to the Android and iOS versions.

What we collect

Nothing. Origami Wars does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.

Microphone

The game can use your microphone for one feature: blowing into the mic sends a gust of wind across the screen. The game asks for permission before it uses the microphone, and you can say no or switch the feature off in Settings. The sound is checked on your device, as it happens, only to tell whether you are blowing. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Data kept on your device

Your scores, achievements, unlocked skills and settings are saved on your device so the game remembers them. They never leave your device. Deleting the game deletes them.

Sharing

The share buttons open your device's own share options. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share it.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Contact

Breakthrough Games. For questions about this policy or the game, open an issue on GitHub.
