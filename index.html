Paper Wars — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Paper Wars (formerly Origami Wars) is made by Breakthrough Games. This policy explains what the game does with your information.

What we collect

The game itself collects nothing. It has no accounts and no analytics, and we never see, store or sell your personal information.

Advertising

On Android the game can show a rewarded video, only when you choose to watch one (for an extra life, or for a weapon when you restart). These videos come from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, an approximate location from your IP address, and information about how ads are shown and used, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where the law asks for it, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change that choice later. How Google uses this information. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Buying the game

You can buy the game once through Google Play to play without videos. Google Play handles the payment; the game only learns whether the purchase is owned, and never sees your payment details.

Microphone

The game can use your microphone for one feature: blowing into the mic sends a gust of wind across the screen. The game asks for permission before it uses the microphone, and you can say no or switch the feature off in Settings. The sound is checked on your device, as it happens, only to tell whether you are blowing. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Data kept on your device

Your scores, achievements, unlocked skills, settings and whether you bought the game are saved on your device so the game remembers them. They never leave your device. Deleting the game deletes them.

Sharing

The share buttons open your device's own share options. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. The game itself collects no information from anyone; the ads described above are provided by Google.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
